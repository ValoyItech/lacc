About Lekgoledi

A women and youth-led rural-based community arts organization pioneering the development of the rural creative and cultural arts sector in Limpopo.

Who We Are

Lekgoledi Arts and Cultural Centre (LACC) is a women and youth-led rural based community arts organization operating in Limpopo Province, Lepelle Nkumpi Municipality.

We continue to pioneer the development and growth of the rural creative and cultural arts sector through increased involvement in a number of arts, culture and heritage projects both at community and public school level.

In addition to increasing accessibility and growth of the arts in Limpopo, LACC has embarked on an innovative strategy to collaborate and partner with reputable national and international artists, arts organizations and government departments to drive success across key projects ranging from visual arts, animation, performing arts, festivals and music development portfolios.

  • Community-led development
  • Youth and women empowerment
  • Cultural heritage preservation
  • Rural creative economy growth

Our Mission & Vision

Guided by purpose, driven by community, and anchored in culture.

Our Mission

To pioneer the development of the rural creative and cultural arts sector by increasing accessibility to the arts, building strategic partnerships, and delivering innovative arts, culture and heritage projects that empower women and youth in rural Limpopo.

  • Provide platforms for musicians and performing artists to showcase talent
  • Enhance artists' confidence and public performance skills
  • Contribute to job creation and income generation for local artists
  • Promote cultural heritage and tourism destinations

Our Vision

To establish Limpopo as a leading hub for rural creative arts and cultural heritage, where every rural artist has access to resources, training, and markets to build a sustainable livelihood through their craft.

  • A thriving rural creative economy across Capricorn and Sekhukhune
  • Preserved and celebrated indigenous knowledge and traditions
  • Youth equipped with 4IR skills in animation, coding and digital arts
  • Social change driven through arts-based advocacy and awareness
