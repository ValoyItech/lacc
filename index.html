Preserving Heritage.
Empowering Communities through Arts

Lekgoledi Arts & Cultural Centre is a women and youth-led rural-based community arts organization in Lebowakgomo, Limpopo, pioneering the development of the rural creative and cultural arts sector.

Our Core Focus Areas

Six pillars that define our mission and impact across Limpopo.

01

Community Research

In-depth research and projects that address the real needs of rural artists and cultural practitioners. We conduct participatory research to map cultural assets, document indigenous knowledge, and co-design interventions with communities.

Participatory Community-led
02

Cultural Heritage

Preserving and promoting Sepedi and Bapedi heritage through arts, storytelling, music, dance, and traditional knowledge. We work with elders and cultural practitioners to document oral histories and revive endangered practices.

Oral history Intergenerational
03

Youth Skills Training

Platforms for youth skills training in animation, coding, performing arts, and business development for sustainable livelihoods. Our 4-month programs combine technical skills with entrepreneurial training and mentorship.

4IR skills Mentorship
04

Collaborative Events

Organizing regional cultural events, carnivals, and the annual Sepedi Cultural Heritage Festival to showcase local talent. We co-create events with traditional authorities, municipalities, and artists.

Annual festival Tourism
05

Artistic Expression

Creating safe spaces for visual arts, poetry, theatre, music, and digital animation for emerging rural artists. We run artist residencies, open mic nights, and digital labs where rural artists can experiment and collaborate.

Residencies Networking
06

Social Impact

Addressing critical social issues like Gender-Based Violence through creative arts, workshops, and community campaigns. We use arts-based approaches to tackle GBV, HIV/AIDS stigma, and youth unemployment.

Awareness Advocacy

Sepedi Cultural Heritage Festival 2026

The 4th Annual Sepedi Cultural Heritage Festival expands into the Limpopo Cultural Heritage Festival, celebrating Bapedi, Vha-Tsonga, Vha-Venda, and Ndebele cultures.

Join us on 25 September 2026 at the Bapedi Royal Kingdom in Sekhukhune for music, dance, poetry, visual arts, craft demonstrations, and heritage tourism.
