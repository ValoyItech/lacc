From build-up events across Capricorn and Sekhukhune to the grand Heritage Festival finale - join us in celebrating Limpopo's rich cultural tapestry.

25 September 2026

Limpopo Cultural Heritage Festival 2026

The 4th Annual Sepedi Cultural Heritage Festival expands into the Limpopo Cultural Heritage Festival, celebrating Bapedi, Vha-Tsonga, Vha-Venda, and Ndebele cultures. Five build-up events lead to the grand finale at Bapedi Royal Kingdom.

Festival Build-Up Events

A series of monthly events leading to the grand finale on Heritage Day, co-hosted with Traditional Authorities across Limpopo.

Main Event
25 September 2026 Bapedi Royal Kingdom, Mohlaletse 2 Days

Main Closing Festival - Cultural Heritage Celebration

The grand finale celebrating Bapedi, Vha-Tsonga, Vha-Venda and Ndebele cultures. Two days of cultural dance groups, brass band and live band performances, indigenous music, poetry, wedding step groups, Tjate Heritage Site tours, crafters and visual artists.

Cultural Dance Brass Bands Indigenous Music Poetry Heritage Tours Visual Arts
Hosted with Bapedi Royal Kingdom, COGHSTA & Dept of Tourism
01 Build Up 1
Capricorn District

Traditional & Contemporary Dance Showcase

Cultural dance groups, brass band music performance, poetry, wedding step groups, instrumental musicians, crafters and visual artists. Includes a Bakone Malapa Open Museum tour and Tjate Heritage site tour.

Dance Brass Band Poetry Heritage Tour
Co-hosted by Mphahlele Traditional Authority
02 Build Up 2
Sekhukhune District

Bapedi Music Showcase

Indigenous music, brass bands, live performances, and workshops on the business of arts. A celebration of Bapedi musical heritage featuring both traditional and contemporary artists.

Indigenous Music Brass Bands Live Bands Business Workshop
Co-hosted by Zebediela Traditional Authority
03 Build Up 3
Mafefe Traditional Authority

Poetry, Storytelling & Visual Arts

Spoken word performances, traditional storytelling sessions, and visual arts exhibitions by local crafters. An inter-generational platform for cultural knowledge exchange.

Spoken Word Storytelling Visual Arts Crafters
Co-hosted by Mafefe Traditional Authority
04 Build Up 4
Roka Phasha

Khwiting ya Noka Art-Tourism Event

A unique fusion of art and tourism promoting cultural heritage destinations and rural lodges. Showcasing how arts and culture can drive village tourism and local economic development.

Art-Tourism Heritage Sites Rural Lodges Cultural Destinations
Co-hosted under Roka Phasha Traditional Authority
